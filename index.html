Broward County DMV Appointment Guide: How to Schedule Online

As Florida’s second-most populous county with nearly 2 million residents, Broward County experiences extremely high demand for motor vehicle and driver’s license services. Whether you live in Fort Lauderdale, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Hollywood, Pompano Beach, or Coral Springs, visiting a local service center without an advance reservation can result in long wait times or being turned away due to capacity limits.

To streamline customer service, the Broward County Tax Collector and state FLHSMV offices enforce an appointment-preferred system. By booking a DMV Appointment broward online, drivers can reserve a guaranteed time slot for driver’s license renewals, REAL ID upgrades, vehicle titling, and driving tests.

📍 Broward Notice: Due to heavy traffic at high-volume centers like Lauderdale Lakes and Miramar, walk-in services are strictly limited. Online scheduling prior to your visit is strongly advised.

Key DMV Service Center Locations in Broward County

Broward County features several full-service driver’s license centers and tax collector offices strategically distributed across the region. Knowing which facility handles your specific request is crucial:

  • Lauderdale Lakes Full-Service Center: Located on State Road 7, this is one of Broward's largest centers handling driver licenses, REAL ID, and CDL testing.
  • Miramar Service Center: Serving South Broward residents (Miramar, Pembroke Pines, Hollywood), offering full licensing and road test evaluations.
  • Pompano Beach Service Center: Convenient for North Broward residents needing driver's license renewals, ID cards, and motor vehicle titling.
  • Deerfield Beach Branch: Handles vehicle registration, tag renewals, disabled parking permits, and title transfers.
  • Sunrise / Sawgrass Branch: Primary location for tag and title transactions, vessel registrations, and property tax payments.

Step-by-Step: Booking a DMV Appointment Broward Online

Reserving your time slot takes less than five minutes if you follow these simple instructions:

  1. Access the Booking Portal: Open the online portal at DMV Appointment broward to access Broward County branch listings.
  2. Select Service Category: Choose between "Driver License / ID Services" or "Motor Vehicle Title & Tag."
  3. Pick Your Broward Location: Select the service center nearest to your zip code (e.g., Lauderdale Lakes, Miramar, or Pompano Beach).
  4. Choose Date and Time: Browse the appointment calendar for open time slots. Early morning hours (8:30 AM to 10:00 AM) generally offer the quickest check-in times.
  5. Enter Applicant Details: Provide your legal name, email address, and phone number to receive instant SMS and email confirmations.

Services Provided at Broward County Offices

Depending on whether you visit a state-operated FLHSMV location or a Broward County Tax Collector branch, you can complete the following transactions:

1. Driver’s License & REAL ID Compliance

First-time Florida licenses, out-of-state driver's license transfers, replacement of lost or stolen cards, and upgrading to a federal REAL ID (gold star card) require physical in-person appointments.

2. Written Knowledge & Driving Road Tests

Teens or first-time drivers taking the Class E written exam or behind-the-wheel driving test must book a specific test slot in advance.

3. Vehicle Titling, Tags & Registrations

Transferring an out-of-state car title to Florida, issuing new license plate tags, renewing annual registration stickers, and paying sales tax are handled at tax collector locations across Broward.

Required Documents for Your Broward DMV Appointment

To avoid being turned away upon arrival, gather original, physical documents prior to your appointment time:

Proof of Identity

  • Original U.S. Birth Certificate OR unexpired U.S. Passport.
  • For non-citizens: Permanent Resident Card or foreign passport with valid I-94.

Proof of Social Security Number (SSN)

  • Original Social Security Card, W-2 form, or 1099 statement showing full SSN.

Two Proofs of Broward County Residential Address

  • FPL (Florida Power & Light) utility bill dated within 60 days.
  • Broward County residential lease agreement, mortgage statement, or property tax bill.
  • Florida vehicle registration or bank statement displaying your Broward address.

Pro Tips for Securing Appointments in Broward County

Due to dense population levels in South Florida, appointment slots in Broward County can book out weeks in advance. Use these insider tips to find earlier availability:

  • Check Early in the Morning: The online scheduling system recycles canceled slots daily between 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM. Refreshing the portal at these times often reveals same-week openings.
  • Consider Adjacent County Offices: Florida permits residents to utilize tax collector offices in adjacent counties. If Broward offices are fully booked, check availability in neighboring Palm Beach or Miami-Dade counties.
  • Complete Eligible Tasks Online: Routine tag renewals and eligible driver's license renewals can be completed online via GoRenew without stepping foot inside an office.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I walk into a Broward County DMV office without an appointment?

While limited standby walk-ins may be accepted early in the morning at select locations, wait times can exceed 3 to 4 hours, and service is not guaranteed if daily capacity is reached.

What is the busiest DMV office in Broward County?

The Lauderdale Lakes and Miramar service centers generally experience the highest volume of daily visitors due to their central locations and full-service capabilities.

Do I need to live in Broward County to use a Broward DMV office?

No. Florida residents holding a valid Florida address can utilize driver license and vehicle titling services at any authorized County Tax Collector office across the state.
